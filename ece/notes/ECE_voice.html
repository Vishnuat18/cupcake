Active and Passive Voice

Unit II: Grammar and Sentence Structure (Study Notes)

Voice indicates whether the subject of a sentence performs the action (Active Voice) or receives the action (Passive Voice).

  • Active Voice: Subject + Verb + Object (e.g., "The teacher teaches the students.")
  • Passive Voice: Object + be-verb + Past Participle (V3) + by + Subject (e.g., "The students are taught by the teacher.")

Rule for Conversion

The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence; the verb changes to 'be-verb + past participle (V3)'; and 'by + agent/subject' may be added at the end.

Shortcut – Convert in 3 moves:
1. The Object of the active sentence jumps to the front and becomes the new subject.
2. The Verb changes to the matching form of 'be' + past participle (V3). Tense of 'be' must match the original tense.
3. 'by + old Subject' goes at the end.

Examples

Active Voice Passive Voice
Ram writes a letter. A letter is written by Ram.
The cat chased the mouse. The mouse was chased by the cat.

Voice – Exercise Questions

Part A – Fill in the Blanks

Provide the correct active or passive verb form.

  1. Active: She cooks food. → Passive: Food ______________ by her. (is cooked/was cooked)
  2. Active: They built the bridge. → Passive: The bridge ______________ by them. (was built/is built)
  3. Passive: The book was read by him. → Active: He ______________ the book. (read)
  4. Active: The manager will sign the letter. → Passive: The letter ______________ by the manager. (will be signed)

Answers:

  1. is cooked
  2. was built
  3. read
  4. will be signed

Part B – Choose the Best Option

Select the correct sentence construction.

  1. Choose the correct passive form of 'She cooks food.'
    a) Food cooked by her. b) Food is cooked by her. c) Food was cooking by her. d) Food cooks by her.
  2. Choose the correct passive form of 'They built the bridge.'
    a) The bridge is built by them. b) The bridge built by them. c) The bridge was built by them. d) The bridge builds by them.
  3. Choose the correct active form of 'The mouse was chased by the cat.'
    a) The cat chases the mouse. b) The cat chased the mouse. c) The cat is chasing the mouse. d) The cat will chase the mouse.

Answers:

  1. b) Food is cooked by her.
  2. c) The bridge was built by them.
  3. b) The cat chased the mouse.

Part C – Convert the Voice

Convert the following sentences as instructed.

  1. Change into passive voice: 'The manager will sign the letter.'
  2. Change into active voice: 'The homework was completed by the students.'
  3. Change into passive voice: 'The chef is preparing dinner.'

Answers:

  1. The letter will be signed by the manager.
  2. The students completed the homework.
  3. Dinner is being prepared by the chef.
