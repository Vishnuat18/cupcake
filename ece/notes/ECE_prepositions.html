Prepositions

Unit II: Grammar and Sentence Structure (Study Notes)

A preposition is a word placed before a noun, pronoun, or noun phrase to show its relationship with another word in the sentence.

Examples:

  • The book is on the table.
  • She went to college.
  • He came from Chennai.
  • The students are in the classroom.

Types of Prepositions

1. Prepositions of Time

The main prepositions of time are:

At (Specific time)
Examples: at 5 o'clock, at noon, at midnight, at night
Sentence: The train arrives at 6 p.m.
On (Days and dates)
Examples: on Monday, on Sunday, on 15 August, on my birthday
Sentence: We have an examination on Monday.
In (Months, years, seasons, centuries, and longer periods)
Examples: in January, in 2026, in summer, in the 21st century
Sentence: He was born in 2005.
Easy Distinction: At → exact time | On → day/date | In → month/year/long period

2. Prepositions of Place

At (Specific place or point)
Examples: at the bus stop, at the station, at the door, at school
Sentence: She is waiting at the bus stop.
In (Inside an area, place, or enclosed space)
Examples: in the room, in the box, in Chennai, in India
Sentence: The students are in the classroom.
On (On a surface)
Examples: on the table, on the wall, on the floor, on the roof
Sentence: The mobile is on the table.

3. Prepositions of Direction and Movement

To (Movement toward a place/person): She went to the library. (go to school, travel to Chennai, give it to Ravi)
From (Starting point): He came from Salem. (from Chennai, from school, from Monday)
Into (Movement from outside to inside): He walked into the room.
Out of (Movement from inside to outside): She came out of the house.
Through (Movement from one side to another through an interior space): We walked through the forest.
Across (Movement from one side to the other side): He walked across the road.
Along (Movement following the length or direction of something): They walked along the river.

4. Prepositions of Position

Above (Higher position): The fan is above the table.
Below (Lower position): The temperature is below 10°C.
Under (Directly beneath something): The cat is under the table.
Over (Directly above or covering): The plane flew over the city.
Between (For two people, things, or places): The school is between the bank and the hospital.
Among (For more than two people or things): She was sitting among her friends.
Behind (At the back of something): The car is behind the building.
In front of (At the front position): He is standing in front of the gate.
Near (At a short distance): The hospital is near my house.
Beside (Next to someone or something): She sat beside her mother.

5. Prepositions of Duration

For (Indicates a period of time)
Examples: for two hours, for five days, for three years
Sentence: He has lived here for five years.
Since (Used with a starting point of time)
Examples: since Monday, since 2020, since morning
Sentence: She has been working here since 2020.
Important: For → duration | Since → starting point

6. Prepositions of Relationship / Association

With (Association, company, instrument, or possession): She went with her friends. / He wrote with a pen.
Without (Absence): He left without his bag.
By (Doer in passive voice or method/means): The letter was written by Ravi. / We travelled by train.

7. Prepositions of Reason / Purpose

For (Purpose or intended use): This gift is for you. / Medicine is for treatment.
Because of (Reason): The match was cancelled because of rain.
Due to (Reason, usually in formal usage): The flight was delayed due to bad weather.
