Idioms and Phrases

Unit I: Vocabulary and Verbal Ability (Study Notes)

An idiom is a group of words whose meaning cannot be understood from the literal meaning of the individual words. Idioms add colour and expressiveness to language.

A phrase is a small group of words that gives a particular meaning, but unlike a sentence, it does not contain a subject and a verb together.

Common Idioms & Meanings

Idiom Meaning & Usage
Break the ice To start a conversation in a social setting.
"He told a joke to break the ice."
Once in a blue moon Very rarely.
"We go out for dinner once in a blue moon."
Hit the books To study hard.
"I need to hit the books tonight for my exam."
A piece of cake Something very easy to do.
"The test was a piece of cake."
Burn the midnight oil To work or study late into the night.
"She had to burn the midnight oil to finish the project."
Under the weather Feeling slightly ill.
"I am feeling a bit under the weather today."

Idioms & Phrases – Exercise

Part A – Fill in the Blanks

Complete the sentences using appropriate idioms from the study notes.

  1. She was ______________ and stayed home from college.
  2. Passing that easy quiz was ______________ for him.
  3. We only meet our cousins ______________.
  4. He had to ______________ before the final exam.

Answers:

  1. under the weather
  2. a piece of cake
  3. once in a blue moon
  4. hit the books

Part B – Choose the Best Answer

Select the correct meaning of the idiom.

  1. 'Break the ice' means:
    a) To break something fragile b) To start a conversation c) To end a friendship d) To feel cold
  2. 'Burn the midnight oil' means:
    a) To waste fuel b) To sleep early c) To work/study late at night d) To light a lamp
  3. Choose the idiom that means 'very rarely':
    a) A piece of cake b) Once in a blue moon c) Hit the books d) Break the ice

Answers:

  1. b) To start a conversation
  2. c) To work/study late at night
  3. b) Once in a blue moon

Part C – True or False

Indicate whether the following statements are True or False.

  1. 'A piece of cake' refers to something difficult to do. (True / False)
  2. 'Under the weather' means feeling slightly ill. (True / False)
  3. 'Hit the books' means to damage a book. (True / False)
  4. 'Break the ice' is used to describe starting a conversation. (True / False)

Answers:

  1. False
  2. True
  3. False
