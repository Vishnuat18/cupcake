Tenses

Unit II: Grammar and Sentence Structure (Study Notes)

Tenses show the time of an action.

There are 3 main tenses:

  • Present Tense
  • Past Tense
  • Future Tense

Each tense has 4 forms: Simple, Continuous, Perfect, and Perfect Continuous. So, there are a total of 12 tenses.

A. PRESENT TENSE

1. Simple Present Tense

Use: Used for habits, routines, general truths, facts, and repeated actions.

Active Formula: Subject + V1 + Object (He/She/It → V1 + s/es)
Passive Formula: Object + am/is/are + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She writes a letter.
Passive: A letter is written by her.
Active: They play cricket.
Passive: Cricket is played by them.

2. Present Continuous Tense

Use: Used for an action happening now or around the present time.

Active Formula: Subject + am/is/are + V1-ing + Object
Passive Formula: Object + am/is/are + being + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She is writing a letter.
Passive: A letter is being written by her.
Active: They are building a house.
Passive: A house is being built by them.

3. Present Perfect Tense

Use: Used for an action that has been completed recently or whose result is connected with the present.

Active Formula: Subject + has/have + V3 + Object
Passive Formula: Object + has/have + been + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She has written a letter.
Passive: A letter has been written by her.
Active: They have completed the work.
Passive: The work has been completed by them.

4. Present Perfect Continuous Tense

Use: Used for an action that began in the past and is continuing up to the present.

Active Formula: Subject + has/have + been + V1-ing + Object + since/for
Passive: Generally not used in standard English.

Example:

Active: She has been writing a letter for two hours.

B. PAST TENSE

5. Simple Past Tense

Use: Used for an action completed in the past.

Active Formula: Subject + V2 + Object
Passive Formula: Object + was/were + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She wrote a letter.
Passive: A letter was written by her.
Active: They completed the work.
Passive: The work was completed by them.

6. Past Continuous Tense

Use: Used for an action that was continuing at a particular time in the past.

Active Formula: Subject + was/were + V1-ing + Object
Passive Formula: Object + was/were + being + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She was writing a letter.
Passive: A letter was being written by her.
Active: They were repairing the car.
Passive: The car was being repaired by them.

7. Past Perfect Tense

Use: Used for an action completed before another past action.

Active Formula: Subject + had + V3 + Object
Passive Formula: Object + had + been + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She had written a letter.
Passive: A letter had been written by her.
Active: They had completed the work.
Passive: The work had been completed by them.

8. Past Perfect Continuous Tense

Use: Used for an action that continued for some time before another past action.

Active Formula: Subject + had + been + V1-ing + Object + since/for
Passive: Generally not used in standard English.

Example:

Active: She had been writing a letter for two hours.

C. FUTURE TENSE

9. Simple Future Tense

Use: Used for future actions, decisions, promises, predictions, etc.

Active Formula: Subject + will + V1 + Object
Passive Formula: Object + will + be + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She will write a letter.
Passive: A letter will be written by her.
Active: They will complete the work.
Passive: The work will be completed by them.

10. Future Continuous Tense

Use: Used for an action that will be continuing at a particular time in the future.

Active Formula: Subject + will + be + V1-ing + Object
Passive: Not normally used in standard English.

Example:

Active: She will be writing a letter at 5 p.m.

11. Future Perfect Tense

Use: Used for an action that will be completed before a specific future time or another future action.

Active Formula: Subject + will + have + V3 + Object
Passive Formula: Object + will + have + been + V3 + by + Subject

Examples:

Active: She will have written a letter.
Passive: A letter will have been written by her.

12. Future Perfect Continuous Tense

Use: Used for an action that will have continued for a period of time up to a specific point in the future.

Active Formula: Subject + will + have + been + V1-ing + Object + since/for
Passive: Generally not used in standard English.

Example:

Active: She will have been writing for two hours.

Tenses – Exercise Questions

Part A – Fill in the Blanks

Choose the correct form of the verb given in brackets.

  1. She _______ to college every day. (go)
  2. They _______ dinner when I arrived. (have)
  3. He _______ the work before the manager came. (complete)
  4. I _______ this book twice. (read)
  5. We _______ to Chennai next week. (travel)
  6. The children _______ in the garden now. (play)
  7. She _______ here for five years. (work)
  8. By next month, he _______ the project. (finish)

Answers:

  1. goes
  2. were having
  3. had completed
  4. have read
  5. will travel
  6. are playing
  7. has been working
  8. will have finished

Part B – Choose the Correct Option

Select the right tense construction for the following statements.

  1. She _______ a letter yesterday.
    a) writes b) wrote c) has written d) is writing
  2. They _______ the match before it started raining.
    a) finish b) finished c) had finished d) have finished
  3. He _______ TV at the moment.
    a) watches b) watched c) is watching d) has watched
  4. I _______ my homework already.
    a) complete b) completed c) have completed d) am completing
  5. She _______ her examination next month.
    a) writes b) wrote c) will write d) has written

Answers:

  1. b) wrote
  2. c) had finished
  3. c) is watching
  4. c) have completed
  5. c) will write

Part C – Identify the Tense

Identify the tense of the underlined verb.

  1. She is cooking dinner.
  2. They visited Delhi last year.
  3. He has completed the assignment.
  4. We will attend the meeting tomorrow.
  5. She had left before I arrived.
  6. They have been working since morning.

Answers:

  1. Present Continuous
  2. Simple Past
  3. Present Perfect
  4. Simple Future
  5. Past Perfect
  6. Present Perfect Continuous

Part D – Active to Passive Voice

Change the following sentences into passive voice.

  1. The teacher explains the lesson.
  2. She wrote a letter.
  3. They are building a bridge.
  4. He has completed the project.
  5. The police arrested the thief.
  6. She will finish the work.

Answers:

  1. The lesson is explained by the teacher.
  2. A letter was written by her.
  3. A bridge is being built by them.
  4. The project has been completed by him.
  5. The thief was arrested by the police.
  6. The work will be finished by her.

  2. The work is completed by the students.
  3. The house is being painted by the workers.
  4. The project has been completed by her.

Answers:

  2. The students complete the work.
  3. The workers are painting the house.
  4. She has completed the project.
  5. Thousands of people will watch the match.
