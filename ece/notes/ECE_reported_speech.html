Reported Speech

Unit II: Grammar and Sentence Structure (Study Notes)

Direct Speech reports the exact words spoken by a person, enclosed in quotation marks.

Indirect (Reported) Speech conveys the meaning of what was said without quoting the exact words.

Connectors based on Sentence Type

  • Use that for statements.
  • Use if / whether for yes/no questions.
  • Use the question word itself (what, where, why) for wh-questions.

Key Changes While Converting

1. Remove Quotes & Use Connector

He said, "I am tired." → He said that he was tired.

2. Change Pronouns

Adjust according to the speaker and reporter.
"I am happy," she said. → She said that she was happy. ('I' becomes 'she')

3. Change Tenses (Backshift)

Shift one step back if the reporting verb is in past tense (e.g., said).
"I play" → he said he played | "I will go" → he said he would go.

4. Change Time and Place Expressions

today → that day
here → there
tomorrow → the next day (or the following day)

Shortcut – The 'One Step Back' Rule: When the reporting verb is in the past tense (e.g., said), perform tense backshifts: Present → Past | Past → Past Perfect | 'will' → 'would' | 'can' → 'could'.
Note: General/Universal Truths do not backshift. (e.g., "The earth revolves around the sun" remains unchanged).

Examples

Direct Speech Indirect Speech
She said, "I am happy." She said that she was happy.
He said, "I will go tomorrow." He said that he would go the next day.

Reported Speech – Exercise

Part A – Fill in the Blanks

Provide the correct form of the verbs or phrases inside brackets.

  1. Direct: He said, "I am reading a book." → Indirect: He said that he ______________ a book. (was reading)
  2. Direct: She said, "I went to the market yesterday." → Indirect: She said that she ______________ to the market the day before. (had gone)
  3. Indirect: He said that he was tired. → Direct: He said, "I ______________ tired." (am)
  4. 'Tomorrow' in direct speech becomes ______________ in indirect speech.

Answers:

  1. was reading
  2. had gone
  3. am
  4. the next day (or the following day)

Part B – Choose the Best Option

Select the right choice.

  1. Choose the correct indirect speech for: He said, "I am reading a book."
    a) He said that he is reading a book. b) He said that he was reading a book. c) He said that he read a book. d) He said that he reads a book.
  2. Which connector is used to report a yes/no question?
    a) that b) if/whether c) because d) since
  3. 'Here' in direct speech usually becomes _______ in indirect speech.
    a) there b) here c) where d) that place

Answers:

  1. b) He said that he was reading a book.
  2. b) if/whether
  3. a) there

Part C – Convert the Speech

Convert the sentences into direct or indirect speech.

  1. Change into indirect speech: The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
  2. Change into direct speech: She said that she would come the next day.
  3. Change into indirect speech: He said, "I have finished my homework."

Answers:

  1. The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun.
    (Note: Universal facts/truths do not backshift)
  2. She said, "I will come tomorrow."
  3. He said that he had finished his homework.
