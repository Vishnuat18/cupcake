Sentence Structure & Error Identification

Unit I: Vocabulary & Unit II: Grammar (Study Notes)

1. Rearranging Jumbled Words

This exercise tests the understanding of correct English sentence structure. A standard English sentence follows the pattern: Subject + Verb + Object (S + V + O).

Time and place expressions usually come at the end of the sentence, in that order (Time, then Manner, then Place is one common guideline, though 'place before time' is also frequent — always read the rearranged sentence aloud to check it sounds natural).

Steps to Rearrange Jumbled Words

  • Identify the subject: Who or what the sentence is about.
  • Identify the verb: The action word or state of being.
  • Identify the object/complement: Who or what receives the action.
  • Place adverbs, adjectives, and prepositional phrases in their natural position.
  • Read aloud: Read the rearranged sentence to check if it sounds correct and natural.
Example:
Jumbled: market / she / vegetables / bought / from / the
Rearranged: She bought vegetables from the market.
Shortcut: First circle the verb (action word) in the jumbled list — it anchors the sentence. Then ask 'Who/What does this action?' to find the subject, and 'To whom/what?' to find the object. Everything else slots around this S-V-O core.

2. Error Correction

Error correction tests the ability to identify and correct grammatical mistakes in a sentence. Common errors occur in tense, subject-verb agreement, articles, prepositions, and word order.

Most exam sentences contain only one deliberate error, so scan tense, then subject-verb agreement, then articles/prepositions in that order — the mistake is usually in one of these three areas.

Shortcut: Read the sentence and ask three quick checks:
1. Does the verb tense match the time word (e.g., yesterday, tomorrow)?
2. Does the verb agree with a singular/plural subject?
3. Is 'a' used before a consonant sound and 'an' before a vowel sound?

Common Types of Errors

Type of Error Example (Incorrect → Correct)
Tense error He go to school yesterday. → He went to school yesterday.
Subject-verb agreement She do her work. → She does her work.
Article error He is a honest man. → He is an honest man.
Preposition error He is good in English. → He is good at English.
Plural/Singular error I have two informations. → I have two pieces of information.

Sentence Structure & Errors – Exercise

Part A – Jumbled Words Fill in the Blanks

Rearrange the jumbled words inside brackets to complete the sentences.

  1. Rearrange and fill: 'quickly / he / runs' → He ______________ quickly.
  2. Rearrange and fill: 'book / reading / is / she / a' → She is reading a ______________.
  3. Rearrange and fill: 'school / to / went / they' → They went ______________ school.
  4. Rearrange and fill: 'letter / wrote / a / he' → He wrote a ______________.

Answers:

  1. runs
  2. book
  3. to
  4. letter

Part B – Jumbled Words Choice

Select the correct rearranged sentence or structure component.

  1. Which is the correctly rearranged sentence for 'homework / every day / his / does / he'?
    a) He does every day his homework. b) He does his homework every day. c) Every day does he his homework. d) His homework every day he does.
  2. Which is the correctly rearranged sentence for 'quickly / ran / boy / the / school / to'?
    a) The boy ran quickly to school. b) Quickly the boy to school ran. c) To school ran the boy quickly. d) The boy to school quickly ran.
  3. In an S+V+O sentence, which part usually comes first?
    a) Object b) Verb c) Subject d) Adverb

Answers:

  1. b) He does his homework every day.
  2. a) The boy ran quickly to school.
  3. c) Subject

Part C – Rearrange the Following

Write the full rearranged sentence.

  1. letter / a / wrote / to / friend / she / her
  2. is / today / weather / pleasant / the / very
  3. carefully / the / read / instructions / students / the

Answers:

  1. She wrote a letter to her friend.
  2. The weather is very pleasant today.
  3. The students read the instructions carefully.

Part D – Error Correction Fill in the Blanks

Provide the grammatically correct word to fix the sentence error.

  1. 'He don't like tea' → He __________ like tea.
  2. 'She is a MA student' → She is __________ MA student.
  3. 'Each of the boys have a bag' → Each of the boys __________ a bag.
  4. 'He is married with a doctor' → He is married __________ a doctor.

Answers:

  1. doesn't
  2. an
  3. has
  4. to

Part E – Error Correction Choice

Identify the correct sentence structure.

  1. Identify the correct sentence:
    a) He go to school yesterday. b) He goes to school yesterday. c) He went to school yesterday. d) He going to school yesterday.
  2. Identify the correct sentence:
    a) She do her work. b) She does her work. c) She doing her work. d) She did do her work.
  3. Choose the correct article: 'He is ___ honest man.'
    a) a b) an c) the d) no article needed

Answers:

  1. c) He went to school yesterday.
  2. b) She does her work.
  3. b) an

Part F – Identify and Correct

Identify the error and write the correct sentence.

  1. 'I have two informations about the topic.'
  2. 'She don't know the answer.'
  3. 'He is good in Mathematics.'

Answers:

  1. I have two pieces of information about the topic. (or 'some information')
  2. She doesn't know the answer.
  3. He is good at Mathematics.
